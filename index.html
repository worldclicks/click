01 — The concept

The simplest action
a person can take.

A click is simple, immediate and accessible to almost everyone on Earth.

CLICK starts with a universal action. WorldClick turns that action into a shared experience where individual participation contributes to a collective global picture — people, communities and countries visible on the same board.

One person presses a button. A country sees its score move. A season crowns a winner. The smallest digital gesture becomes a way to belong to something larger.

  1. i.

    A universal action

    No tutorial, no barrier. Pressing a button is the most democratic interaction in the digital world.

  2. ii.

    A shared scoreboard

    Individual clicks aggregate into country scores and rankings — a picture painted by millions of small gestures.

  3. iii.

    A collective story

    Seasons, tournaments and impact initiatives turn repeated participation into an ongoing global narrative.

02 — How it works

From one press
to a global picture.

  1. 01

    Participate

    Open WorldClick and press the button. That is the entire onboarding.

  2. 02

    Accumulate points

    Your activity is recorded as application-level points attached to your participation.

  3. 03

    Contribute to rankings

    Your participation feeds leaderboards and your country's score.

  4. 04

    Join seasons & competitions

    Take part in time-boxed seasons and tournaments run under published rules.

  5. 05

    Become part of the experience

    A global participation experience shared by people, communities and countries.

Important distinction

Ordinary clicks are application-level events. They do not automatically generate blockchain transactions, CLICK tokens, revenue, donations or buybacks.

WorldClick may organize seasons, rankings and tournaments based on published rules. Each season is a chapter: scores reset, countries compete again, and the story continues.

  • Time-boxed global seasons with clear start and end conditions.
  • Rankings and country scores under transparent, published rules.
  • Tournaments with defined structures and reward allocations.

Rewards, where offered, follow published rules. No prize is guaranteed, and ordinary clicks do not themselves earn tokens.

08 — Market launch

Price discovery,
by design.

CLICK plans to use Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC) for market launch and price discovery, with an allocation of 150,000,000 CLICK. The DBC pool will use one selected quote mint. Accepting SOL and USDC in the Public Offering does not mean the DBC pool accepts both as simultaneous quote assets. The final DBC configuration remains subject to publication.

09 — Buyback & Burn

Conditional,
never promised.

Where eligible net revenue is generated and received, the project may allocate a defined portion to token buybacks and burns under a transparent, published policy.

  • Buybacks may only use eligible net revenue actually received by the project.
  • They are never guaranteed, and no fixed percentage has been approved.
  • Clicks do not automatically trigger buybacks or burns.

10 — Impact & Philanthropy

Participation
with a purpose.

Ten percent of the maximum supply is reserved for impact initiatives. The vision: a game the whole world can play should leave something behind in the real world.

This is a token allocation reserved for impact initiatives — not a guaranteed fiat donation. Clicks do not automatically generate donations. Charitable partners, beneficiaries and amounts will be announced only when confirmed.
